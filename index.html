How can I reduce my AWS bill?

Start with Cost Explorer grouped by service, then check for idle and unattached resources, oversized instances, missing Savings Plans, NAT Gateway data charges, S3 storage classes and log retention. The free playbook has the commands.

Can you migrate my servers or application to AWS?

Yes. A migration starts with an assessment and target architecture, then a staged cutover with a tested rollback. It works for on-premises servers, VPS hosts, Heroku and other clouds.
